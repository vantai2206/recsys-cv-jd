TIN TUYEN DUNG

Vi tri: DevOps Engineer
Cong ty: Cong ty Cong nghe DEF
Dia diem: Ha Noi (hybrid)
Muc luong: thoa thuan theo nang luc

MO TA CONG VIEC
Chung toi can mot DevOps Engineer tham gia xay dung va van hanh ha tang cho cac
he thong microservice dang phuc vu hon mot trieu nguoi dung. Ban se lam viec truc
tiep voi doi phat trien backend de tu dong hoa toan bo quy trinh tu commit den
production.

YEU CAU BAT BUOC
- Thanh thao Python de viet cac script tu dong hoa va cong cu noi bo.
- Kinh nghiem dong goi ung dung bang Docker va trien khai tren cum Kubernetes.
- Da tung xay dung pipeline CI/CD (GitHub Actions, Jenkins hoac GitLab CI).
- Biet quan ly ha tang bang Terraform theo huong Infrastructure as Code.
- Kinh nghiem lam viec voi Amazon Web Services: EC2, S3, Lambda.
- Thanh thao moi truong Linux va viet shell script.
- Su dung Git trong quy trinh lam viec nhom.
- Biet truy van SQL de ho tro dieu tra su co lien quan den co so du lieu.

YEU CAU UU TIEN
- Da tung thiet lap he thong giam sat voi Prometheus va Grafana.
- Quen voi phuong phap lam viec Agile/Scrum.

QUYEN LOI
Bao hiem suc khoe, 15 ngay phep nam, ngan sach dao tao 20 trieu moi nam.
